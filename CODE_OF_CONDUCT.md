# Code of conduct

Skimless is reviewed by people who are often tired. Be the reviewer you want on your own pull request.

- Critique the change, not the person.
- Assume the author shipped the diff in good faith.
- No harassment, slurs, or sexualized comments. No piling on.
- Disagreement about a heuristic is welcome. "This rule is noisy, here is a diff that shows it" is the useful shape.

Maintainers can edit, lock, or hide comments that break this, and can block people who keep doing it. If you need to report a maintainer, use the security contact in SECURITY.md.
